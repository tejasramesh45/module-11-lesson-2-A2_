Welcome to the Park

Get ready for heart-pounding roller coasters, water rides, and interactive attractions. Whether you are seeking high-speed thrills or relaxed family fun, we have something for everyone!

Popular Attractions

  • Thunder Coaster: Reaches speeds up to 80 mph with 5 inversions.
  • Splash Falls: A refreshing water slide with a 50-foot plunge!
  • Sky Wheel: Enjoy breathtaking 360-degree views of the entire park.
